# Screening and full-text assessment

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `scopus_records.csv` | All 271 Scopus records with bibliographic metadata (no abstracts) and the screening decision. `note` marks the 8 records retained at the second-pass check of excluded titles and the 3 records that are conference proceedings volumes rather than studies. |
| `ssrn_retained_records.csv` | The 46 SSRN records retained at title and abstract screening (of 320 identified). |
| `arxiv_retained_records.csv` | The 58 arXiv records retained at title and abstract screening (of 177 identified). |
| `fulltext_assessment.csv` | Every report assessed in full text or sought for retrieval: the 153 database reports remaining after deduplication and the 27 records identified through other methods. For each report: outcome (`included`, `excluded`, `not retrieved`), exclusion code (EC1–EC7, Table 2.1 of the review), reason, and the identifier of the study in `3_extraction/coding_sheet.csv`. |

Notes:

- For SSRN and arXiv the screening log records only the retained records; the excluded ones (274 and 119) can be regenerated by re-running the queries in `1_search/search_strategy.md`, but results may differ slightly because both repositories are updated continuously.
- Duplicates (24) were removed after screening, by DOI and normalised title; `source_after_deduplication` reports the source kept for each report.
- Two reports describe the same study (Bühler et al. 2020a, 2020b) and are counted once among the included studies.
- Theses excluded under EC5 are listed in `fulltext_assessment.csv` for completeness but are not cited in the review.
- Scopus data are reproduced only to the extent needed to document the selection (identifiers and basic metadata). Complete database exports, including abstracts, are not redistributed.
